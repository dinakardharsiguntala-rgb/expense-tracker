iPhone Automation Setup

Pre-Configured By Us • Only iOS Permission Needed

⚡ 1-Tap Quick Setup Auto-Created

Tap below to copy your pre-configured URL and automatically open Apple Shortcuts to create the automation:

⚠️ Critical Apple Rule: In Apple Shortcuts, the "Message Contains" field only accepts ONE SINGLE WORD (e.g. debited). Do NOT separate multiple words with commas, or Apple Shortcuts will wait for the entire phrase and will NEVER run!

3-Step Setup (Only 2 Minutes)

1

Create Automation ➔ Message

In Shortcuts app, tap Automation tab ➔ tap + ➔ choose Message.

• In Message Contains: enter only debited

(Or leave it blank if selecting your Bank name under Sender)

2

Select "Run Immediately"

Required iOS Permission

Select "Run Immediately" and turn OFF "Notify When Run".

This gives Apple permission to log your expenses automatically in the background without asking every time.

3

Add Action ➔ "Open URLs"

Tap Next ➔ search for action "Open URLs".

1. Paste your private URL:

https://dinakardharsiguntala-rgb.github.io/expense-tracker/?sms=

2. Tap the blue Shortcut Input token above your keyboard so it attaches to the URL!

Tap Done. Your iPhone will now record incoming bank SMS automatically!

Add to iPhone Home Screen

Activate Fullscreen & Apple Shortcuts Integration

Why this is needed on iPhone: Adding this PWA to your Home Screen enables iOS standalone mode. Apple Shortcuts can then open and sync your bank SMS directly into your app in the background without opening browser tabs!
1

Tap the "Share" Button in Safari

At the bottom toolbar of Safari on your iPhone, tap the Share icon (square with an arrow pointing up ⎋ or 📤).

2

Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down the options and tap "Add to Home Screen" (icon with a plus sign ➕).

3

Tap "Add" in Top-Right Corner

Confirm the name (Expenses) and tap Add in the top-right corner.

4

Open "Expenses" from Home Screen

Now open the app from your Home Screen! It runs full-screen, stores data privately on your phone, and works seamlessly with Apple Shortcuts.
